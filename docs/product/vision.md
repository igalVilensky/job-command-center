# Product Vision

Job Command Center is a self-hosted AI job search automation dashboard for developers and job seekers.

The product helps users turn messy job information into an actionable job search pipeline.

## Problem

Job seekers receive opportunities from many channels: job alert emails, job board recommendations, recruiter messages, LinkedIn messages, manually found job posts, referrals, and application replies.

This information is scattered, repetitive, noisy, and hard to prioritize.

## Goal

Create a personal command center that helps a user:

1. collect job opportunities
2. normalize them into structured records
3. analyze fit against an editable candidate profile
4. decide what to apply to
5. track application progress
6. remember follow-ups and next actions
7. use AI as a helper without losing human control

## Positioning

Job Command Center is open-source, self-hosted, local-first for development, AI-provider-agnostic, human-in-the-loop, privacy-conscious, and useful without AI keys through mock mode.

It is not a hosted SaaS, a job scraper, an auto-apply bot, or a replacement for human judgment.

## Product promise

Bring all your job opportunities into one place, let AI help you triage them, and keep full control over your data, decisions, and applications.
